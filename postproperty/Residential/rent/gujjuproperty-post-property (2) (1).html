Questions owners ask us

Is it really free, or is there a catch later?

Listing is free and stays free. We take no brokerage and no commission when the property sells or rents. We do sell optional paid services — a rent agreement, a title check, an assisted plan — but you only pay if you choose one, and your listing works exactly the same if you never do.

Who gets to see my phone number?

Nobody, until you say so. Interested buyers send a request, you see who they are, and you release your number to the ones you want to talk to. You can stop sharing at any point.

How do you keep brokers out?

Every account is verified, and accounts that behave like brokers — bulk enquiries, repeated numbers, listings posted on behalf of others — get flagged and blocked. It isn't perfect, so there is a "Listed by broker" report button on every listing, and we act on those within 24 hours.

How long does a listing stay up?

Until you close the deal or take it down. We will nudge you every few weeks to confirm it is still available, because stale listings waste everyone's time — including yours.

Can I list a plot or a shop, not just a flat?

Yes. Pick Commercial for shops, offices, warehouses and coworking, or Land / Plot for residential plots, farmland and NA plots including Dholera SIR. The form changes to ask the right questions for each.
